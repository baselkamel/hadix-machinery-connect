==================================================

COMPANY DESCRIPTION

==================================================

AL•HADIX provides industrial machinery and engineering consultancy.

Our main role is to help companies that need to purchase industrial machinery.

When a company has a machinery requirement, AL•HADIX:

- Understands the customer's requirement

- Helps identify suitable machinery solutions

- Connects the customer with suitable machinery manufacturers or suppliers through our professional connections

- Provides engineering and technical consultancy

- Provides access to technical specialists when required

- Supports testing and commissioning when required

- Provides technical advice and support related to machinery

We are a technical intermediary and engineering consultancy.

AL•HADIX is NOT:

- A machine manufacturer

- A construction company

- A general contractor

- An installation company

DO NOT include:

- Construction services

- Contracting services

- Installation services

- Project management services

- Projects / portfolio pages

MAIN SERVICES

==================================================

Use these as the main services:

Providing engineering and technical guidance based on machinery requirements and applications.

4. Technical Evaluation

Helping evaluate machinery solutions based on technical requirements, application and suitability.

5. Testing & Commissioning

Providing access to technical specialists when specific expertise is required.

Providing technical advice related to machinery performance, operation and optimization.
